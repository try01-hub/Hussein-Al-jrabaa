About

I studied cyber and information security, and I've ended up working where security and money meet: the systems that move, screen and settle bank payments.

At Arab Bank I support the Treasury and Sanctions applications. When something breaks I trace it through application logs, SQL results and integration errors, across the application, database, middleware, operating system and network layers, and I work with the owners of each layer until it's fixed.

At EastNets I installed and configured PaymentSafe for banks, connected it to T24, Flexcube and Temenos, set up IIS and Tomcat servers, built UAT environments and helped deliver a CBPR+ (ISO 20022) rollout. I write down what I learn so the next person can fix it faster.
